Identity fraud detection

Catch fake identities before they sign up.

Every signup is checked by five fraud modules. Each one adds risk points, and the total decides: approve, review or block, always with the reasons.

How it works

  1. A signup arrives
    Name, email, phone, plus hidden signals like how fast the form was filled.
  2. Five checks run
    Email, bot, phone, duplicate and impersonation checks each look for their own kind of fraud.
  3. Points add up
    Every suspicious sign has a point value. The total is capped at 100.
  4. A decision is made
    The result is saved with all its reasons and shown to the admin on the dashboard.

The five modules

Email check

Catches fake, throw-away and random-looking emails.

  • Invalid email format+35
  • Disposable domain (mailinator...)+30
  • Random-looking username+20
  • Role address (admin@, support@)+15

Bot check

Catches scripts pretending to be people.

  • Hidden honeypot field filled+60
  • Form sent in under 2 seconds+40
  • Script user-agent (curl, python)+40
  • Many signups from one IP+10 / +30
  • Machine-looking name+20

Phone check

Catches invented phone numbers.

  • Looks fake (9999999999, 9876543210)+35
  • Wrong length or not a mobile number+30
  • No phone given+5

Duplicate check

Catches the same value typed into two different fields.

  • Email and phone are identical+50
  • Email and name are identical+30
  • Phone and name are identical+30

Impersonation check

Catches people pretending to be someone else.

  • Reserved role or famous name+100
  • Authority word inside the name+75
  • Typo of a reserved name+70 to 80
  • Disguised copy of a real user+50

Why rules and not machine learning?

Every decision can be explained in plain words, no training data is needed, and the weights are easy to tune. The design is modular: a machine learning model could be added later as one more module, without changing the others.
